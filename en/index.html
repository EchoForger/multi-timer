01 / MULTI

Timers and Pomodoro, each with its own rhythm.

Countdowns, stopwatches, Pomodoro, settings, and statistics share one fixed-width menu bar panel. A 24-hour timeline shows when every focus session started, ended, and whether it was completed.

It feels like part of macOS.

A native AppKit interface, system notifications, and updates that understand how the app was installed. New releases show their notes and let you update now, be reminded later, or skip that version.

Your time is not telemetry.

No account and no telemetry; data stays local by default. Pomodoro history stores only start time, end time, and completion status; daily statistics are derived live.

MultiTimer follows the macOS appearance automatically. Buttons and timer colors use your system accent—blue when your Mac is blue, green when you switch to green.

Install MultiTimer

Two simple ways.
Pick your favorite.

01 Recommended

Download the DMG

Open the installer and drag MultiTimer into your Applications folder.

Download latest ↓ macOS Ventura or later
02 Homebrew

Install from Terminal

Two commands install the app. Homebrew can handle future updates too.

Terminal 
$ brew tap EchoForger/tap
$ brew install --cask multi-timer

If macOS blocks the first launch, Control-click MultiTimer and choose Open. After launch, the app checks GitHub Releases for updates and asks before installing; you can also use Check for Updates at the bottom of the panel.
